# MindQuest – Content Generation Flow

MindQuest uses a structured creative pipeline to generate high-quality, educational, and entertaining podcast episodes for kids.  
Here is the full flow:

---

## 1. Select a Learning Theme

- Choose a broad theme such as **history**, **engineering**, **math**, **the human body**, **space**, or any other knowledge domain.  
- Each chosen theme becomes the foundation for a **self-contained mini-series**.

---

## 2. Build a Mini-Series Plan

MindQuest automatically generates a mini-series outline containing the most exciting, surprising, and age-appropriate topics.

**Examples:**

- **History:** Ancient Egypt, famous explorers, invention of writing  
- **Math:** Zero, infinity, prime numbers, geometry in nature  
- **Human Body:** Heart, brain, senses, digestion  

Every topic is designed to spark curiosity and encourage exploration.

---

## 3. Generate Episode Scripts

For each topic, MindQuest creates a complete podcast script starring two core characters:

### **The Professor**
- Calm, wise, and warm  
- Speaks slowly and clearly  
- Explains everything from first principles  
- Breaks complex ideas into simple, visual explanations  
- Acts as the mentor and guide  

### **Pinocchio**
- Naive, curious, funny, and full of childlike wonder  
- Asks smart Socratic-style questions  
- Represents the listener’s curiosity  
- Adds energy and humor  

Together, they create an engaging conversation that feels alive, fun, and educational.

---

## 4. Voice Generation (TTS)

Once the script is ready, MindQuest converts it into audio using two TTS voices:

- **Professor voice:** warm, articulate, calm, clear  
- **Kid voice:** playful, energetic, natural  

The final result is a polished, child-friendly episode ready for publishing.
